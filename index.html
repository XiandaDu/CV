About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Engineering with an Option in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Waterloo (CGPA 90.87/100). My research spans Information Retrieval & Search Agents, Vision-Language Model Evaluation, and Generative Models. I am currently an Autonomy and Algorithm Engineer Intern at Waabi, working on novel view synthesis quality evaluation for self-driving simulation.

I have had the privilege of working with Prof. Wenhu Chen and Max Ku on image evaluation, Prof. Amir-Hossein Karimi and Ahmed Abdelaal on generative priors for inverse problems, Prof. Jian-Yun Nie and Yuxing Tian (Université de Montréal) on search agents, Prof. Renée Sieber (McGill University) and Prof. Fengran Mo (Rochester Institute of Technology) on retrieval benchmarks, and Prof. En-Hui Yang on adversarial robustness.

I am actively seeking graduate and research positions in retrieval, multimodal AI, and generative modeling.
